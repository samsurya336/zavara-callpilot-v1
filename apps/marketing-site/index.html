The problem

Most teams learn to sell on real prospects.

A Bengaluru SaaS rep pitches a CTO on data residency at 11 am and defends annual pricing to a Pune CFO at 4 pm. Three ways to prepare, each with a cost.

Roleplay with a manager

Depends on someone else's calendar. Rarely repeatable, never consistent.

Learning on live calls

One fumbled objection in front of a real buyer can cost a qualified lead.

Scripts and battlecards

Knowing the answer on paper is not saying it confidently when a founder interrupts you.

How it works

Pick. Talk. Review. Retry.

The whole learning loop fits in one sitting. Practise a scenario, read what went wrong, retry the moment that mattered, and go again until it feels natural.

What your team learns

STEP 1

Pick a challenge

Start an Open Challenge with a ready-made fictional company, or upload one PDF about your product and generate Knowledge-based Challenges.

STEP 2

Talk to AI buyers

One-on-One or a Boardroom of CEO, CTO and CFO. Choose Assist for private hints or Battle for none.

STEP 3

Review your report

Scores by skill, objections you handled, questions you missed, every point tied to a line in your transcript.

STEP 4

Retry with Time Machine

FAQ

Questions sales leaders ask us

Does CallPilot AI join my real customer calls?

Not yet. Today CallPilot AI is a practice environment with simulated buyers. Real-time assistance on live customer calls is planned and marked Coming Soon.

Do I need to upload product documents to start?

No. Open Challenges work immediately with ready-made fictional companies. Uploading one PDF or text document is optional and unlocks Knowledge-based Challenges, product-aware hints and product accuracy checks.

Who can see my uploaded documents and my sessions?

Knowledge Base entries, sessions and reports belong to the account that created them. The AI buyers do not silently read your document. They learn product facts only when you say them in the conversation.

Is Time Machine a third practice mode?

No. The two modes are Assist and Battle. Time Machine happens after a completed call: pick one of your answers and branch a new conversation from that point.

Do the scores predict whether a real customer will buy?

No. Scores and simulated outcomes describe how you performed inside a training simulation. They are not forecasts of real purchasing decisions or revenue.

Is there a limit on how much I can practise?

Each account has a practice allowance for voice sessions, shown in the app. Time Machine retries count towards the same allowance.
